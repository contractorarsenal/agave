Meet the Owner

Jorge Villegas

Owner & Lead Welder  •  Licensed, Bonded & WABO Certified

Agave Welding & Railings LLC is owned and operated by Jorge Villegas. Jorge built this company on the belief that good welding work doesn't need to be complicated — show up, do it right, and leave the job better than you found it.

From custom stair railings for Seattle homeowners to structural repairs on commercial properties, Jorge handles every job with the same attention to detail. If it's not built right, it doesn't go out.

Every weld Jorge does is backed by his WABO certification, Washington state contractor license, and years of hands-on field experience across residential and commercial projects throughout the Puget Sound.

Certifications & Licensing

The Credentials That Matter on the Jobsite

Not every welder is licensed for every type of work. Here's what Agave Welding brings to your project.

WABO Certified

Washington Association of Building Officials structural welding certification. Required for projects where welds must meet WA state building code.

WA State Licensed

Licensed contractor in Washington state. We can pull permits, work with inspectors, and sign off on work that requires a licensed contractor.

Bonded & Insured

Bonded for both residential and commercial work. We carry appropriate liability coverage for every project, regardless of size.

OSHA Trained

Safety trained for commercial jobsites. Ready to work alongside general contractors and property management teams without extra coordination overhead.

How We Work

The Way Agave Welding Does Business

We don't chase volume. We take on the right projects, do them correctly, and make sure every client knows exactly what they're getting before we start.

Straight Communication

You get a real answer within 24 hours. No vague estimates, no runaround. If we need more info to price it accurately, we'll tell you what we need.

Built to Code

Every structural weld meets WABO certification standards. Every railing meets Washington state safety code. We don't cut corners on what matters.
