Eastside Custom Work

Bellevue and Kirkland Projects Demand Clean Finishes and Precise Fabrication

Eastside homes — especially newer builds and high-end remodels in Bellevue, Kirkland, Medina, and Mercer Island — tend to call for modern railing systems: clean cable runs, frameless glass panels, or custom steel with brushed finishes. These aren't standard contractor jobs. They require accurate fabrication, tight tolerances, and installers who understand what the end result is supposed to look like.

We work with homeowners, interior designers, and GCs on these kinds of projects regularly. We fabricate the metalwork — posts, frames, cable hardware, glass channels, custom brackets — to spec, and we install it so the finish lines are clean and the welds don't show unless you want them to.

WABO certification covers any structural welding in the scope. Licensed and bonded throughout Washington state.
