What We Do

Welding & Fabrication Across the Puget Sound

From structural welds to custom railings, we take on projects other contractors pass on. Licensed, certified, and built for Washington's commercial and residential market.

Structural Welding

WABO-certified structural welds for commercial buildings, remodels, and residential projects. Meets WA building code. Built for the long haul.

Custom Fabrication

Steel, aluminum, and stainless fabrication built to your specs. Brackets, frames, supports, and custom metalwork — from raw material to finished piece.

Railings & Handrails

Custom steel, aluminum, and cable railings for decks, stairs, and balconies. Code-compliant, built tough for Seattle's weather and waterfront conditions.

Welding Repairs

Broken welds, cracked frames, rusted handrails, failed structural connections. We diagnose and repair right the first time — no band-aids.

Mobile Welding

Commercial Projects
