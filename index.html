What is WABO structural welding certification and why does it matter?

WABO stands for Washington Association of Building Officials, and WABO structural welding certification is the highest standard for structural welding in Washington State. To become certified, a welder must pass practical weld tests administered by WABO-approved inspectors. Test welds are cut and pulled to verify they meet or exceed AWS D1.1 Structural Welding Code requirements — meaning the certification verifies actual weld integrity, not just knowledge. WABO certification covers SMAW (stick welding), GMAW (MIG), FCAW (flux-core arc welding), and GTAW (TIG) for both shop and field environments. For property owners and contractors, hiring a WABO-certified welder matters for two reasons: it ensures structural welds will pass city and county inspections, and it protects against liability — a non-certified weld on a load-bearing structure can create serious safety and legal exposure. Agave Welding is WABO certified.
