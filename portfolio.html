Our Work

Recent Projects Throughout the Greater Seattle Area

Commercial Guardrails — Edmonds, WA

Structural Welding • Powder Coat Finish

A commercial property manager needed code-compliant exterior guardrails for an elevated walkway before a city inspection. We fabricated welded steel posts and horizontal infill to IBC commercial height requirements, powder coated flat black, and installed in a single day. The completed railings passed inspection without revisions. Materials: A36 structural steel tube. Finish: powder coat, flat black.

Gym Equipment Modification — Seattle, WA

Commercial Fabrication • Same-Week Turnaround

A Seattle commercial gym needed its weight rack system reconfigured to accept a new equipment layout — requiring precision cuts and full-penetration welds on load-bearing steel that would continue handling significant dynamic loads daily. We assessed the existing structure, cut the required sections, and re-welded with WABO-certified technique including proper preheat and interpass temperature control. Completed within the same week to minimize equipment downtime. Finish: primer and touch-up paint matched to original.

Custom Steel Stair Railings — Greater Seattle Area

Residential Railings • Satin Black Powder Coat

A homeowner needed stair railings to replace deteriorated wood balusters on their main stair. They wanted a clean, modern look with vertical flat-bar pickets — no horizontal members. We fabricated a floor-mounted system with a 1.5" round top rail and 1/2" flat vertical pickets at 4" on center, meeting IRC residential baluster spacing code. Installed in a single day. Materials: A36 steel tube and flat bar. Finish: powder coat, satin black.

Structural Steel Staircase — Everett, WA

Structural Fabrication • Commercial Tenant Improvement

A general contractor required a structural steel staircase for a commercial warehouse conversion — a straight-run stair connecting a new mezzanine to the ground floor. We fabricated C-channel stringers to the engineer's riser-and-run specifications, welded pan-style treads to accept concrete fill, and fabricated connection plates to match the engineer's anchor bolt pattern. WABO-certified welds used throughout for building inspection compliance. Materials: C8×11.5 structural channel, 14-gauge pan treads. Finish: primer.
