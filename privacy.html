Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Agave Welding & Railings LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and handles information when you visit agavewelding.com or contact us through our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website or submit our contact form, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • ZIP code
  • Project description
  • IP address and browser data (via analytics tools)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your quote request or project inquiry
  • Contact you about your welding project
  • Send follow-up communications if you have opted in

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Contact Form Submissions

Our contact form is powered by Formspree (formspree.io). By submitting the form, your information is transmitted to Formspree's servers and forwarded to us. Formspree's privacy policy governs how they handle your data and can be found at formspree.io.

4. Communications Consent

By submitting our contact form, you consent to receive communications from Agave Welding & Railings LLC by phone, text message, or email regarding your project inquiry. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any text message or by contacting us directly.

5. Cookies and Analytics

This website may use cookies or third-party analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site. These tools may collect non-personally identifiable information such as pages visited, time on site, and referring URL. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

6. Third-Party Services

This website uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — loads web fonts
  • Font Awesome (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) — loads icons
  • Formspree (formspree.io) — processes contact form submissions

These services may collect technical information such as your IP address and browser type. Please review their respective privacy policies for details.

7. Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions and associated personal information for a reasonable period to respond to your inquiry and maintain business records. If you would like your information removed, contact us at Agavewelding@gmail.com.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
