Serving Seattle Since Day One

Seattle Has a Lot of Stairs, Decks, and Older Homes That Need Real Railing Work

Seattle's hillside neighborhoods — Queen Anne, Capitol Hill, Magnolia, Beacon Hill — are full of properties with steep grades, tight lots, and stair systems that weren't built to last. We see a lot of decayed handrails, failed post anchors, and railings that have slowly pulled away from the framing. Most homeowners don't notice until someone grabs it and something gives.

We fabricate and install custom steel and aluminum railings that are built to code, properly anchored, and designed for Seattle's wet climate. WABO certification means our structural welds pass Washington state inspection — important for permitted work in Seattle and surrounding jurisdictions.

Beyond railings, we handle structural repairs, commercial welding for Seattle businesses, and mobile welding calls throughout the city and surrounding areas.
