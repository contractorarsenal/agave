Structural Welding

WABO-Certified Structural Welding

Structural welding that meets Washington state building codes and passes inspection. We carry WABO certification for structural welding work — the credential required for commercial buildings, remodels with structural steel, and projects where code-compliant welds are non-negotiable.

Common Projects

  • Structural steel connections on commercial builds
  • Beam and column welding for remodels
  • Load-bearing metalwork on residential additions
  • Structural repairs on aging buildings
  • Code-required welding for permit-required projects
  • AWS D1.1 compliant welding documentation

Welding Repairs

Welding Repairs Done Right — Not Patched

Broken welds, cracked frames, rusted handrails, bent gates, or failed structural connections. We diagnose the actual problem and repair it properly — not just run a bead over the crack and hope it holds. If it needs replacing, we'll tell you. If it can be repaired, we'll do it right.

Common Repairs

  • Broken or failed railing welds
  • Cracked gate frames and hinges
  • Gym equipment frame repairs (racks, sleds, rigs)
  • Industrial and commercial equipment repairs
  • Structural connection repairs
  • Rust-damaged metalwork restoration
  • Boat and marine metal repairs
