WABO-Certified, Licensed & Insured for Commercial Welding Projects

Agave Welding & Railings LLC is a licensed and WABO-certified commercial welding contractor serving businesses, property managers, general contractors, and facility owners throughout Seattle, Everett, Tacoma, and the greater Puget Sound area. Owner Jorge Villegas holds the certifications, training, and insurance required for commercial welding work under Washington State building codes.

Credentials for Commercial Work

Commercial welding projects in Washington State — particularly any work involving structural steel — require welders to hold WABO (Washington Association of Building Officials) structural welding certification. Agave Welding is WABO certified. In addition, we hold:

  • OSHA 10-Hour Construction Safety certification — required on many commercial job sites
  • Mobile Elevating Work Platform (MEWP) certification — Group B Type 3, for work at elevation
  • Licensed and bonded as a Washington State contractor
  • General liability insurance — COI available on request
