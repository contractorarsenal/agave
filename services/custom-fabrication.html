What Is Custom Metal Fabrication?

Custom metal fabrication is the process of cutting, bending, and welding steel, iron, or other metals to create a specific structure or component from raw materials. Unlike off-the-shelf products, fabricated metalwork is built to your exact measurements, design, and functional requirements. This makes it the right solution when standard parts don't fit your space, when you need a specific aesthetic, or when code-compliant structural strength is required.

Our Process

Every fabrication project begins with a consultation — either by phone or on-site visit — where we discuss your design intent, material preferences, finish options, and installation requirements. We provide a detailed written estimate before any work begins. Once approved, fabrication takes place in our shop to precise tolerances. We then deliver and install the finished piece, confirming it is level, secure, and code-compliant.

Materials and Finishes

We primarily work with mild steel, stainless steel, and aluminum depending on project requirements. For finishing, we offer powder coating in a wide range of colors and textures for long-lasting corrosion protection, along with primer and paint finishes where powder coating is not required. All exterior metalwork receives rust-inhibiting treatment as standard practice.

Why WABO-Certified Fabrication Matters

Many welding projects — especially anything structural — require certified welds to pass building inspection. Agave Welding holds WABO (Washington Association of Building Officials) structural welding certification, the highest welding credential in Washington State. Our welds are tested to meet and exceed AWS D1.1 Structural Welding Code, meaning every piece we fabricate is built to last and built to pass inspection.
