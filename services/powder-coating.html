Powder Coating Services in Seattle, WA

Durable, Corrosion-Resistant Finishes for Metal Fabrications

Agave Welding & Railings LLC offers professional powder coating services for metal fabrication projects throughout Seattle, Everett, Tacoma, and the greater Puget Sound area. Powder coating provides a durable, attractive, and corrosion-resistant finish that outperforms traditional paint in Seattle's wet climate — making it the preferred finish for outdoor railings, gates, furniture, and structural metalwork.

What Is Powder Coating?

Powder coating is a dry finishing process in which electrostatically charged pigment powder is applied to a metal surface and then cured under heat, bonding the powder into a smooth, hard coating. Unlike liquid paint, powder coating does not require solvents, produces minimal VOC emissions, and creates a finish that is significantly more durable and resistant to chipping, scratching, fading, and corrosion. For metal fabrications in the Pacific Northwest — where salt air, rain, and temperature cycling accelerate corrosion — powder coating is the right choice.

Colors and Textures Available

We offer a wide selection of powder coating options:

  • Smooth gloss finishes in any standard RAL color
  • Matte and satin finishes for a lower-profile aesthetic
  • Hammertone and textured finishes for visual depth
  • Wrinkle textures for an industrial or vintage appearance
  • Flat, gloss, and satin black — the most popular finish for railings and gates
  • Custom colors available on request for architectural color matching

Surface Preparation

Proper surface preparation is critical to powder coating adhesion and longevity. All pieces receive mechanical cleaning and degreasing before coating. For previously painted or coated surfaces, we strip back to bare metal. For new fabrications leaving our shop, we weld, grind smooth, and coat in the same production run — ensuring no exposed bare steel.

Common Applications

We powder coat a wide range of fabricated metalwork:

  • Interior and exterior railings and handrails
  • Driveway gates, garden gates, and fence panels
  • Steel furniture: tables, chairs, shelving, and frames
  • Architectural metalwork: wall fixtures, decorative panels, signage frames
  • Equipment brackets and industrial components
  • Trailer hitches, automotive accessories, and vehicle components

Powder Coating vs. Paint: Why It Matters in Seattle

Seattle's climate is hard on exterior metal finishes. Standard paint fades, chips, and allows moisture intrusion at scratches, leading to rust propagation under the coating. Powder coating's hard, continuous bond prevents this. A properly powder-coated railing or gate can maintain its finish and corrosion resistance for 15–20 years with minimal maintenance, compared to 3–5 years for exterior paint.

Combined Fabrication and Coating

When you work with Agave Welding for custom fabrication, we handle both the welding and powder coating in a single project, so you receive a finished piece ready for installation.
