Custom Railings & Handrails in Seattle, WA

Code-Compliant Steel Railings for Residential & Commercial Properties

Agave Welding & Railings LLC designs and installs custom metal railings and handrails for residential and commercial properties throughout Seattle, Everett, Tacoma, and the greater Puget Sound region. From steel stair railings and cable railings to ADA-compliant handrails and deck guards, every installation is fabricated in-house and installed by WABO-certified welder Jorge Villegas.

Types of Railings We Install

Stair Railings

Interior and exterior stair railings in steel, iron, and mixed materials. We fabricate straight runs, curved railings, and multi-level systems to match your space and style. All stair railings are built to IBC and IRC code specifications for baluster spacing, top rail height, and graspability.

Cable Railings

Horizontal or vertical stainless steel cable systems for decks, patios, and stairs. Cable railings provide an open, modern aesthetic while maintaining code-required infill. We fabricate custom steel posts and source marine-grade cable hardware rated for Washington's coastal climate.

Deck Guards & Guardrails

Code-compliant guardrails for elevated decks, balconies, mezzanines, and landings. All guards are built to the minimum 36" or 42" height requirements depending on occupancy type, with infill that meets the 4" sphere rule.

ADA Handrails

Wall-mounted and post-mounted handrails for stairs, ramps, and accessible routes. We fabricate graspable rail profiles per IBC and ADA requirements and anchor them to masonry, concrete, and wood substrates with the appropriate hardware.

Commercial Railings

Railings for commercial buildings, apartment complexes, retail spaces, offices, and gyms. We have completed commercial railing installations in Edmonds, Seattle, and Everett and can provide documentation to support permit applications.

Custom Iron & Steel Railings

Decorative fabricated iron and steel railings with custom baluster designs and finishes. We work from client sketches or photos to produce one-of-a-kind railings that complement your home's or building's architecture.

Design and Fabrication Process

The process starts with a site visit where we measure the space, assess substrate conditions, and discuss style and code requirements. We provide a written estimate that includes materials, fabrication, finish, and installation. All railings are fabricated in our shop to exact measurements, then installed and confirmed level and code-compliant on-site. Most residential railing projects are complete within one to two weeks from quote approval.

Finishes and Coatings

All railings can be powder coated in a wide range of standard and custom colors. Powder coating provides superior corrosion resistance compared to paint — critical for Seattle's wet climate. We also offer primer-and-paint finishes and clear protective coatings for natural steel aesthetics.

Code Compliance

All railing installations by Agave Welding are designed and built to comply with the International Building Code (IBC), International Residential Code (IRC), and ADA accessibility standards as applicable. WABO-certified welds ensure structural connections meet Washington State requirements.

Serving Greater Seattle

We serve residential and commercial clients throughout Seattle, Everett, Tacoma, Edmonds, Lynnwood, Bellevue, Redmond, Shoreline, Bothell, Arlington, Marysville, and surrounding areas in King, Snohomish, and Pierce Counties.
