What Is Structural Steel Welding?

Structural steel welding involves joining steel members that carry loads — beams, columns, brackets, connections, base plates, and similar components. Unlike decorative or light fabrication, structural welding must meet strict engineering standards because failures can compromise the integrity of a building or structure. In Washington State, structural welds on commercial projects must be performed by WABO-certified welders to pass building inspection.

WABO Certification: What It Means for Your Project

WABO certification is not just a credential — it is a requirement for structural welding on commercial projects in Washington State. To earn WABO certification, a welder must pass practical tests where sample welds are physically pulled and cut to verify compliance with AWS codes. This tests actual weld quality, not just procedural knowledge. When you hire Agave Welding for structural work, you get welds that have already been proven to meet the standard.
