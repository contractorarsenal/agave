Welding Repair Services in Seattle, WA

Professional Weld Repairs for Gates, Railings, Structural Steel & Equipment

Agave Welding & Railings LLC provides professional welding repair services for residential and commercial clients throughout Seattle, Everett, Tacoma, and surrounding communities. Whether you have a broken gate, cracked structural weld, failing railing, or damaged equipment, our WABO-certified welder Jorge Villegas can assess, repair, and restore your metalwork to full strength and function.

Common Welding Repairs We Handle

Gate & Fence Repairs

Broken weld joints, bent frames, cracked posts, torn hinges, and damaged hardware on driveway gates, garden gates, security fences, and decorative iron fencing.

Railing & Handrail Repairs

Broken balusters, cracked welds at post bases, failed wall anchors, and loose handrail fittings. We match existing metal profiles and finishes wherever possible.

Structural Steel Repairs

Cracked welds, corroded connections, failed beam-to-column joints, and damaged gussets on load-bearing steel. All structural repairs are WABO-certified, meeting AWS D1.1 code.

Equipment & Machinery

Broken frame welds on industrial equipment, cracked brackets, failed mounts, and damaged material handling equipment. We repair in your facility or pick up smaller items for shop repair.

Automotive & Trailer Frames

Cracked trailer tongues, broken hitch receivers, frame cracks, damaged steel bumpers, and custom modifications to vehicle frames and truck beds.

Stair Stringers & Treads

Cracked or loose stair stringers, failed weld connections at landings, and broken tread supports. Damaged stair welds are a liability — we repair them promptly and correctly.

Our Repair Process

We start with a site assessment — either in person or based on photos you send — to evaluate the failure mode and determine the repair approach. For welding repairs, understanding why the weld failed is as important as fixing it. We'll identify whether the failure was from fatigue, overload, improper original welds, corrosion, or impact, and address the root cause in the repair. After welding, we grind, clean, and prime or powder coat the repair area to protect against future corrosion.

When to Call Instead of Wait

Welded connections that are cracking or failing tend to worsen under continued load. A minor crack in a gate hinge or stair stringer today can become a complete structural failure later. If you've noticed movement, cracking sounds, visible rust propagating from a weld, or visible separation at a joint, contact us promptly. We provide same-day response and can usually schedule a repair visit within the week.

Quality & Certification

Every welding repair by Agave Welding is performed to the same standard as new fabrication — WABO-certified processes, proper preheat and interpass control for thicker steel, and visually clean final welds. We don't patch; we restore.
